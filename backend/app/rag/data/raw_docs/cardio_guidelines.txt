Cardio Guidelines for Sustainable Fitness

Cardio should feel supportive, not punishing. The goal is to improve heart health, stamina, and daily energy without overwhelming your body.

Types of Beginner-Friendly Cardio  
• Walking
• Cycling
• Elliptical
• Light jogging
• Low-impact aerobics

Walking is the most accessible and effective option for most beginners.

Intensity Levels  
Use the “talk test”:
• If you can talk comfortably → light intensity
• If you can talk but feel slightly breathless → moderate intensity
• If talking is difficult → high intensity (avoid for beginners)

Weekly Structure  
Aim for:
• 2–4 sessions
• 15–30 minutes each

Consistency matters more than duration.

Low-Impact Options  
Low-impact cardio protects joints while improving endurance. Great for beginners, heavier individuals, or anyone returning from injury.

Mindset Notes  
Cardio should leave you feeling energized, not drained. Start small and build gradually.
