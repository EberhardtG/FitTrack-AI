Leg Training Fundamentals

Leg training develops strength, stability, athletic performance, and overall health.

Benefits of Leg Training

• Increases lower-body strength
• Improves balance
• Supports calorie expenditure
• Enhances athletic performance

Beginner Leg Exercises

• Bodyweight Squat
• Chair Squat
• Step-Ups
• Glute Bridge

Intermediate Leg Exercises

• Goblet Squat
• Romanian Deadlift
• Walking Lunges
• Leg Press

Sample Beginner Leg Workout

Perform 3 sets of each exercise.

• Bodyweight Squats — 12 reps
• Step-Ups — 10 reps each leg
• Glute Bridges — 15 reps

Rest 60 to 90 seconds between sets.

Focus on full range of motion and proper form.
