Back Training Fundamentals

The back contains several important muscle groups responsible for posture, pulling strength, and spinal support.

Benefits of Back Training

• Improves posture
• Supports shoulder health
• Increases pulling strength
• Helps prevent muscle imbalances

Beginner Back Exercises

• Resistance Band Row
• Seated Cable Row
• Dumbbell Row
• Lat Pulldown

Intermediate Back Exercises

• Pull-ups
• Assisted Pull-ups
• Barbell Rows
• Chest Supported Rows

Sample Beginner Back Workout

Perform 3 sets of each exercise.

• Lat Pulldown — 10 reps
• Dumbbell Row — 10 reps per side
• Seated Cable Row — 12 reps

Rest 60 to 90 seconds between sets.

Maintain a neutral spine throughout all pulling movements.
