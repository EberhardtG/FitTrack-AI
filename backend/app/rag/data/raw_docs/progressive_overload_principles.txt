Progressive Overload: The Engine of Strength Progress

Progressive overload is the gradual increase of stress placed on the body over time. It’s the core principle behind strength training.

Ways to Progress  
• Add 2–5 lbs
• Add 1–2 reps
• Slow down tempo
• Improve range of motion
• Add one extra set
• Reduce rest slightly

Small increases compound into major strength gains.

Beginner-Friendly Overload Examples  
Week 1: 8 reps
Week 2: 10 reps
Week 3: 12 reps
Week 4: Add 5 lbs and return to 8 reps

This creates predictable, safe progress.

Mindset Notes  
Progressive overload should feel gradual, not extreme. You’re building strength, not testing it.
