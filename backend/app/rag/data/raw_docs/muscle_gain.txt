Muscle Gain Fundamentals

Building muscle requires progressive overload, proper nutrition, recovery, and consistency.

Key Principles

• Increase training resistance over time
• Consume adequate protein
• Get sufficient sleep
• Follow a structured workout routine

Muscle Building Exercises

• Squats
• Deadlifts
• Bench Press
• Rows
• Overhead Press

Training Guidelines

• Perform exercises through a full range of motion
• Focus on proper technique
• Train each major muscle group regularly

Muscle growth occurs gradually through repeated training and recovery cycles.
