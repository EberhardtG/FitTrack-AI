Dumbbell Training Starter Guide

Dumbbells are one of the most versatile tools for strength training. They allow for progressive overload, balanced strength development, and joint-friendly movement.

Beginner Dumbbell Movements  
• Goblet squat
• Dumbbell row
• Dumbbell press
• Romanian deadlift
• Farmer carry

Start with light weights (5–15 lbs) and increase gradually.

Sample Dumbbell Routine (12–15 minutes)  
Perform 8–10 reps per movement.

Goblet squat

Dumbbell row

Dumbbell press

Romanian deadlift

Farmer carry — 20–30 seconds

Dumbbell training builds confidence and strength without complexity.
