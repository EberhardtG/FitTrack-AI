Cooldown Principles: Restoring Comfort After Training

Cooldowns help your body transition from exercise back to rest. They reduce stiffness, support recovery, and improve flexibility.

Why Cooldowns Matter  
Cooldowns:

• Lower heart rate gradually
• Reduce muscle tension
• Improve circulation
• Support long-term mobility

Skipping cooldowns can lead to tightness and discomfort.

Effective Cooldown Structure (2–4 minutes)

Gentle stretching

Deep breathing

Slow walking or marching

Beginner-Friendly Cooldown Routine  
• Hamstring stretch — 20–30 seconds
• Chest stretch — 20–30 seconds
• Calf stretch — 20–30 seconds
• Deep breathing — 30 seconds

Cooldowns help your body feel comfortable after training, making it easier to stay consistent.
