Recovery Fundamentals

Recovery allows the body to adapt to training and improve performance.

Benefits of Recovery

• Reduced fatigue
• Improved performance
• Better muscle repair
• Lower injury risk

Recovery Habits

• Sleep 7 to 9 hours per night
• Stay hydrated
• Eat balanced meals
• Manage training volume

Active Recovery Examples

• Walking
• Stretching
• Mobility exercises
• Light cycling

Signs You May Need More Recovery

• Persistent soreness
• Decreased performance
• Fatigue
• Lack of motivation to train

Recovery is a critical part of long-term fitness success.
