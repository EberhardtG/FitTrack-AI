Mobility Basics: Moving Comfortably Through Daily Life

Mobility training improves joint range of motion and reduces stiffness. It’s essential for beginners, desk workers, and anyone wanting to move more freely.

Key Mobility Movements  
• Cat-cow
• Hip circles
• Shoulder rolls
• Thoracic rotations
• Ankle circles

Mobility work supports strength training by improving movement quality.

Sample Mobility Flow (5–7 minutes)  
• Cat-cow — 10 reps
• Hip circles — 20 seconds
• Shoulder rolls — 20 seconds
• Thoracic rotations — 10 reps
• Ankle circles — 20 seconds

Mobility is the foundation of comfortable movement.
