Warm-Up Principles: Preparing Your Body for Safe Movement

Warm-ups are essential for improving performance and reducing injury risk. A good warm-up should feel gentle, intentional, and energizing.

Why Warm-Ups Matter  
Warm-ups increase:

• Blood flow
• Joint lubrication
• Muscle temperature
• Movement readiness

They also help your mind shift into “training mode.”

Effective Warm-Up Structure (3–5 minutes)

Light cardio — marching in place

Joint mobility — arm circles, hip circles

Movement rehearsal — bodyweight squats or hinges

Warm-ups should never feel exhausting. Their purpose is preparation, not fatigue.

Beginner-Friendly Warm-Up Routine  
• March in place — 30 seconds
• Arm circles — 20 seconds
• Hip circles — 20 seconds
• Bodyweight squats — 10 reps

Warm-ups set the tone for a safe, productive workout.
