Chest Training Fundamentals

The chest muscles are responsible for pushing movements and upper-body strength.

Benefits of Chest Training

• Improves pushing strength
• Supports shoulder stability
• Helps with posture
• Contributes to balanced upper-body development

Beginner Chest Exercises

• Push-up
• Incline Push-up
• Dumbbell Bench Press
• Machine Chest Press

Intermediate Chest Exercises

• Barbell Bench Press
• Incline Bench Press
• Dumbbell Fly
• Chest Dips

Sample Beginner Chest Workout

Perform 3 sets of each exercise.

• Push-ups — 8 to 12 reps
• Incline Push-ups — 10 reps
• Dumbbell Bench Press — 10 to 12 reps

Rest 60 to 90 seconds between sets.

Focus on controlled movement and proper technique before increasing weight.
