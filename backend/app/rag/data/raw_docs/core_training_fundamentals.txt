Core Training Fundamentals: Stability Over Crunches

Core training is about stability, not just abdominal exercises. A strong core supports posture, balance, and safe lifting mechanics.

Beginner-Friendly Core Movements  
• Dead bug
• Bird dog
• Plank (short holds)
• Glute bridge
• Side plank (knees down)

These movements strengthen your core without strain.

Sample Core Routine (8–10 minutes)  
• Dead bug — 8 reps per side
• Bird dog — 8 reps per side
• Glute bridge — 12 reps
• Side plank — 10–15 seconds per side

Core training improves daily movement and reduces back discomfort.
