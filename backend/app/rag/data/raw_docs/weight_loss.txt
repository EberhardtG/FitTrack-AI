Weight Loss Fundamentals

Successful weight loss comes from consistency, sustainable habits, and healthy lifestyle choices.

Key Principles

• Maintain a moderate calorie deficit
• Prioritize protein intake
• Stay physically active
• Sleep adequately
• Manage stress

Helpful Strategies

• Track weight trends instead of daily fluctuations
• Walk regularly
• Strength train two to four times per week
• Consume mostly minimally processed foods

Avoid

• Extremely restrictive diets
• Skipping meals regularly
• Excessive exercise volume

Sustainable weight loss is generally gradual and consistent rather than rapid.
