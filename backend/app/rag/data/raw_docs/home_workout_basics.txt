Home Workout Basics: Building Strength Without a Gym

Home workouts are convenient, accessible, and effective. You don’t need equipment or a large space to build strength and improve fitness.

Why Home Workouts Work  
• Zero commute
• Low friction
• Flexible schedule
• Beginner-friendly environment

Home workouts support consistency by removing barriers.

Essential Movements  
• Bodyweight squat
• Wall push-up
• Hip hinge
• Glute bridge
• Band row (optional band)
• Dead bug

These movements train your entire body safely.

Sample Home Routine (12–15 minutes)  
Perform 8–12 reps per movement.

Squat

Wall push-up

Home workouts are simple, scalable, and effective for building foundational strength.
